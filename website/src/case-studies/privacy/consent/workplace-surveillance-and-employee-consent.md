title: "Case Study: Workplace Surveillance and Employee Consent"
tags: ["privacy", "consent"]
---

The 2021 report “[The Constant Boss: Work Under Digital Surveillance](https://datasociety.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/The_Constant_Boss.pdf)” by Aiha Nguyen, director of _Data & Society’s_ Labor Futures Program, examines the consent-related issues with workplace surveillance, particularly of low-wage and hourly workers.

A member of United for Respect, representative of the retail worker advocacy group, told Aiha Nguyen that Walmart managers asked employees to download an app on their personal devices to check inventory and scan misshelved items, neglecting to mention that the app requires camera access and location services, which are constantly active by default.[^47]

[^47]: Aiha Nguyen, _The Constant Boss: Work Under Digital Surveillance_ (Data & Society, 2021), https://datasociety.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/The\_Constant\_Boss.pdf.
