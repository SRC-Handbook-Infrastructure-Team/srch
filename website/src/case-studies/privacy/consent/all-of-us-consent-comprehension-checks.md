---
title: "Case Study: Consent Comprehension Checks in All of Us"
tags: ["privacy", "consent"]
---

The National Institutes of Health (NIH) [All of Us Research Program](https://allofus.nih.gov) implements this kind of competency check in an attempt to ensure individuals consenting to contribute data have the cognitive capacity to make an informed and engaged decision. Check out [All of Us’ description](https://allofus.nih.gov/article/all-us-consent-process) of their consent process or [this assessment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33275082/) published in the journal _AJOB Empirical Bioethics_ to learn more.
