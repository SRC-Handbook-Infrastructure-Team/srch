title: "Case Study: Sleep Apnea Devices and Insurance Providers"
tags: ["privacy", "dataOwnershipSurveillanceCapitalism"]

In 2018, people with sleep apnea who use breathing machines discovered the machines were secretly sending reports detailing their sleep quality and usage of the machine to their insurance providers, and even that their insurers could use this information to [deny payment](https://www.propublica.org/article/you-snooze-you-lose-insurers-make-the-old-adage-literally-true).[^2]

[^2]: Allen, Marshall. 2018\. “You Snooze, You Lose: Insurers Make The Old Adage Literally True.” ProPublica. https://www.propublica.org/article/you-snooze-you-lose-insurers-make-the-old-adage-literally-true.
