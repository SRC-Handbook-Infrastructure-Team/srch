title: "Case Studies: Biometric Authentication and Faster Checkout"
tags: ["privacy", "privacyTradeoffs"]
---

Common examples include fingerprint scanners and Face ID, which allow users to bypass conventional security codes. From 2020 to 2026, [Amazon](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazon-is-ending-its-palm-id-system-for-retail-amazon-one-as-it-closes-physical-stores/) allowed individuals to register their palms for payment of goods at Amazon-owned companies. While this technology reduced friction at checkout, it required users to provide sensitive biometric data to a private company, one that is not legally required to handle the information responsibly in the same ways medical providers are.
